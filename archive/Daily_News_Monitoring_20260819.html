11 Fleet Order Einride strikes deal to add 500 Tesla Semis to its fleet — the largest Tesla Semi deployment disclosed to date. The Swedish freight-tech firm will roll the trucks out across North America over 24 months (from September), serving Amazon and other shippers on corridors in California, Texas, New Jersey, Illinois and Georgia, run on Einride's Saga AI fleet platform and fully financed through third parties. The order is 35% larger than WattEV's 370-truck deal in May and roughly triples Einride's deployed electric fleet. The buyer profile is the story: not a carrier but a freight-tech operator that owns the shipper relationship and buys trucks as fleet inputs — the same 'solution firm picks the vehicle' dynamic now emerging in Korea's autonomous middle-mile market (#10).
